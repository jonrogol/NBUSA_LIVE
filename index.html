Who We Are

Our Story

From friendships forged in adversity to shared triumphs, the story of AWB Group is one of resilience, unity, and collective vision.

1998

Origins

Three Brazilian immigrants met in Japan in 1998, young, ambitious, and driven by the same dream: to create opportunity where few believed it was possible. What began as friendship soon became a shared journey of learning, success, rivalry, and resilience.

2004–2015

Challenges & Rebirth

In the 2000s, each of us built businesses from scratch, sometimes as competitors. The flood of 2004, the financial crisis of 2005–2008, and the 2011 Fukushima earthquake tested everything we had built. From those challenges came strength, humility, and unity. We rebuilt together with renewed purpose, not just for success, but for growth in spirit, discipline, and understanding. From that renewed friendship, we became family.

2024

Continental Expansion

By 2015, we shared a new dream: to bring our connection and vision to the United States and build something without borders. That dream became reality in 2024 with the foundation of New Business USA Inc. Today, NBUSA is more than a company; it is the natural extension of decades of partnership, friendship, and cultural bridges linking Japan, Brazil, and the United States.

"To connect countries, people, and businesses through trust, innovation, and shared growth."
